Playbook A: Contract Migration

Target: Monthly Plans

Trigger Condition: Active customers on Monthly contract segments exhibiting churn probabilities above 60%.

Actions Required:

  • Send automated "Annual Loyalty Upgrade" email campaign offering a 15% discount on Annual terms.
  • Flag account in CRM system with task assign: "Call customer to review contract billing".
  • Present self-service upgrade option on customer account profile settings page.

Playbook B: Satisfaction Recovery

Target: CSAT <= 2

Trigger Condition: Active accounts with Satisfaction Score below 3 or complaints filed within the last 30 days.

Actions Required:

  • Initiate personal customer support executive outreach callback within 24 hours.
  • Establish direct route to premium Support Desk for any subsequent support tickets.
  • Send an executive "issue resolution survey" to confirm corrective measures were met.

Playbook C: VIP Engagement Drive

Target: Champions Segment

Trigger Condition: Active accounts categorized in 'Champions' or 'Loyal Customers' RFM segments with declining platform sessions.

Actions Required:

  • Send invitations to participate in exclusive product feedback panels / beta test releases.
  • Award priority client rewards (e.g., custom account perks, free extension items).
  • Schedule quarterly account partnership reviews (for high-value business clients).

Playbook D: Reactivation Campaigns

Target: Declining Engagement

Trigger Condition: Customers with zero platform log-ins over 14 days and session duration declines exceeding 40%.

Actions Required:

  • Trigger personalized dynamic newsletter displaying highlights of unused features.
  • Offer "Re-engage promo" (e.g. $5 credit or 7-day complimentary feature access).
  • Send targeted push notifications outlining software patches or major new content updates.
